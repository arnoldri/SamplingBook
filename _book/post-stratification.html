8.1 Replicate weights

Later in the course we’ll see situations where non-response means that the weights from our sample design have to be adjusted (e.g. by poststratification). When this happens the estimation methods associated with the sample designs above no longer work, and we have to resort to approximations.

In such circumstances we often create multiple sets of weights in addition to the adjusted weights. These replicate weights are what are used to estimate the standard errors, and they have the advantage that they can create standard errors for a wider range of estimators than the usual means and totals.

Jacknife weights are calculated by systematically deleting PSUs at the first stage of selection, and then scaling the weights on the remaining rows so that they still add up to the same total.
